# AI Statement

This repository documents my progress through the **OSSU Computer Science** curriculum.

Artificial intelligence is an important part of my learning process, but it is **not** a substitute for learning the material myself.

## Why I Use AI

I do not learn particularly well from long lecture videos or by simply reading textbooks. While both have value, I learn far more effectively through interactive discussion, guided problem solving, and immediate feedback.

AI allows me to engage with the material in a way that matches how I learn best. Instead of passively consuming information, I actively work through concepts one question, one exercise, and one idea at a time.

## How I Use AI

Throughout this curriculum, AI serves as a tutor and study partner. Some examples include:

- Explaining concepts from the course in multiple ways.
- Teaching through a Socratic, question-driven approach rather than simply providing answers.
- Generating an unlimited number of practice exercises targeted to concepts I am currently learning.
- Providing hints when I become stuck instead of immediately solving problems for me.
- Helping me review my understanding and identify gaps in my knowledge.
- Assisting with planning, organization, and documentation throughout the curriculum.

## What AI Does **Not** Do

My goal is to learn computer science—not to have AI complete coursework for me.

Whenever possible, I solve assignments and programming exercises myself. AI is used to guide my thinking, answer questions, and provide additional practice, not to bypass the learning process.

If I ask for help with coursework, I prefer conceptual explanations, guiding questions, and incremental hints over complete solutions.

## Why This Statement Exists

AI-assisted learning is a deliberate part of this project, and I believe transparency is important.

Rather than hiding my use of AI, I want to document how it contributes to my education. Used responsibly, AI makes it possible to receive individualized instruction, unlimited practice, and immediate feedback—advantages that are difficult to obtain through traditional self-study alone.

Everything in this repository represents my own learning journey, with AI serving as an educational tool that helps me understand the material more deeply rather than replacing the work of learning itself.
